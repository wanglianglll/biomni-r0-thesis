5.前端启动流程
①启动虚拟环境：conda activate biomni
②source一下环境：source ./4_set_env.sh
③用 conda 环境里的 python 启动：
CONDA_BASE=$(conda info --base)
ENV_PYTHON="$CONDA_BASE/envs/biomni/bin/python"
echo $ENV_PYTHON

④启动 FastAPI：
setsid /bin/bash -lc "cd ~/autodl-tmp/Biomni-main && $ENV_PYTHON -m uvicorn scripts.fastapi_server:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 6006 --workers 1" > ~/autodl-tmp/Biomni-main/logs/fastapi.log 2>&1 &
ps：检查之查看近期日志
tail -n 200 ~/autodl-tmp/Biomni-main/logs/fastapi.log

⑤启动 Gradio前端：
setsid /bin/bash -lc "cd ~/autodl-tmp/Biomni-main && $ENV_PYTHON app.py" > ~/autodl-tmp/Biomni-main/logs/gradio.log 2>&1 &
ps：检查之查看近期日志
tail -n 200 ~/autodl-tmp/Biomni-main/logs/gradio.log

⑥本机测试FastAPI接口：
curl -i -X POST http://127.0.0.1:6006/generate \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"prompt":"Hello, how are you?","max_new_tokens":64,"temperature":0.7}'
